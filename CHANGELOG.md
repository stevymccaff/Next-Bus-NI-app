# What's new

## 1.1.2 (29 September 2026)

- The app icon is tidied up: the bus's rear wheel and the clock are now the same size.
- Includes everything in 1.1.1 below, which was never released separately.

## 1.1.1 (28 September 2026)

- **About** now has a **Privacy policy** button, which opens the privacy policy on this site.
- The stop search and stop code boxes use City Hall as their example ("e.g. City Hall",
  "e.g. 001636"), a stop everyone knows.

## 1.1.0 (28 September 2026)

The first release for friends.

- Home-screen widget for any bus stop in Northern Ireland: search by name, **Near me**, or the
  stop code from the pole.
- Up to four rows per widget, each a route from a stop or "any bus from this stand". Rows shrink
  and then drop off as the widget gets smaller.
- Live countdowns, live delays and cancellations, and service alerts for your routes.
- Pick a badge colour for each row, or let it follow your wallpaper.
- Refreshes about once a minute in your commute times, every 15 minutes otherwise, and never with
  the screen off.
- Times follow your phone's 12/24-hour setting.
- **Help & feedback** in the app fills in a report for you to send by email. After a crash, it
  offers to send a report (nothing is sent unless you choose to).
