# Next Bus NI

[![Support on Ko-fi](https://img.shields.io/badge/Ko--fi-support%20development-FF5E5B?logo=ko-fi&logoColor=white)](https://ko-fi.com/stevymccaff)

A free Android home-screen widget showing the next buses from your stops in Northern Ireland,
with live delays and service alerts. No accounts, no ads, no tracking.

**[How to install it](INSTALL.md)** · [Download the latest version](https://github.com/stevymccaff/Next-Bus-NI-app/releases/latest) ·
[What's new](CHANGELOG.md) · [Privacy policy](privacy.md)

Not affiliated with Translink. Times come from Translink's Journey Planner.

## What it does

- **Any NI bus stop:** search by name, tap **Near me**, or type the stop code from the pole.
- **Up to four rows per widget:** a route from a stop ("G1 → Dundonald"), or "any bus from this
  stand". Mix stops in one widget, and place as many widgets as you like.
- **Live countdowns:** the minutes count down on your home screen. Buses tracked live show how late
  or early they are, and cancelled ones say so.
- **Service alerts** for the routes you show. Tap one to read it.
- **Your colours:** each row's badge follows your wallpaper, or pick one of eight colours.
- **Fits the space:** rows shrink, then drop off the bottom, as you make the widget smaller.
- **Easy on battery and data:** about once a minute in your commute times, every 15 minutes
  otherwise, and never with the screen off.
- Android 8 or later. There's no iPhone version.

## Problems and ideas

In the app, open **Help & feedback** (near the bottom). It fills in the details for you (app
version, phone, your widgets' stops and routes; never your location), and you can change anything
before it's sent. Or email [nextbusni@gmail.com](mailto:nextbusni@gmail.com).
